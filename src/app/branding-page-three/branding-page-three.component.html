Nirab Hossain

Nirab Hossain is a Bangladeshi actor and model who began his career in TV commercials before moving into dramas and films. He made his film debut in 2009 with Mon Jekhane Hridoy Sekhane and later appeared in works like Banglasia, Sheitaan, Abbas, and Casino. Known for his versatility, Nirab has also acted in international projects and joint productions.
