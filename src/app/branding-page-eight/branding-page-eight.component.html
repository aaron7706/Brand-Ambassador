MAMNUN HASAN

EMON

Mamnun Hasan Emon is a Bangladeshi film & television actor who began his career as a model & rose to fame with movies like Daruchini Dip & Gohine Shobdo. He is known for his work in both films & TV dramas & is one of the recognizable faces in Bangladeshi entertainment.
